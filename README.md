# Debbie Flynn & Associates — Website

Official website for **Debbie Flynn & Associates**, lawyers based in Wagga Wagga, NSW, Australia.

## Overview
Debbie Flynn & Associates provides legal services throughout the Riverina region with 20+ years of experience in:
- Family Law
- Conveyancing
- Children’s Court — FACS
- Legal Aid
- Criminal Law & Police Matters
- Wills and Estates
- Property Law & Contracts

## Features
- **Responsive & Modern Design**: Pure HTML5 and custom CSS with a clean glassmorphism aesthetic.
- **Dark/Light Theme Toggle**: Seamless theme switching with local storage memory and system preferences support.
- **SEO & AI Ready**: Includes structured JSON-LD schema (`LegalService`).
- **Optimized Assets**: Responsive background images.
